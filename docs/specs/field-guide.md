Every page was checked on 2026-11-05.

## Sprite style

About 64 × 64 px, about 16 colors per sprite, a dark outline tinted by the part it borders, two or three shades per surface lit from the top left, a three-quarter view. Close to the real species in shape, banding and color; our own art, nothing copied from an existing game. The mascot bees stay as they are. Examples are on the design canvas (board "Field guide: sprite examples").
