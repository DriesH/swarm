Write the pull request for this task. Use plain, simple English. Keep it short. Write what you know from the diff and the commits; do not guess.

The title is one line of at most 72 characters. It says what the change does.

The description has these four sections, in this order, and nothing else:

## What

- One bullet for each change a reviewer sees in the diff.

## Why

- One bullet for each reason the change is needed.

## Notes for reviewers

- One bullet for each thing that needs a careful look, and each choice you made.

## Open tasks

- [ ] One checkbox for each step that is still open, such as a test on staging.

Keep every section, even a short one. Do not add attribution lines, signatures or a summary of your own work.
